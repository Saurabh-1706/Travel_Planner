# Business Requirement Document (BRD)

## 1. Project Overview
**Project Name**: AI Travel Planner
**Objective**: Build a production-ready AI-powered Travel Planner that helps users discover destinations, generate personalized itineraries, book travel services, manage budgets, collaborate with others, and receive intelligent travel recommendations.

## 2. Business Value & Goals
- **Monetization**: Generate revenue through affiliate bookings (flights, hotels, activities), premium features (advanced AI planning), and targeted advertising.
- **User Engagement**: Keep users on the platform throughout their entire travel lifecycle—from inspiration to booking, traveling, and reviewing.
- **Market Differentiation**: Leverage AI to provide hyper-personalized itineraries and smart recommendations, setting the platform apart from traditional static travel booking sites.

## 3. Target Audience (User Personas)
- **The Solo Backpacker**: Needs budget-friendly options, hostel recommendations, and offline maps.
- **The Family Vacationer**: Prioritizes kid-friendly activities, safety, convenient transportation, and spacious accommodations.
- **The Business Traveler**: Needs quick booking, itinerary management, receipt tracking, and proximity to business centers.
- **The Group Trip Organizer**: Needs collaboration tools, expense splitting, and voting mechanisms.

## 4. Key Business Requirements
1. **AI Integration**: The platform must leverage AI for trip generation, hotel/activity recommendations, and an intelligent chat assistant.
2. **Booking Capabilities**: The platform must allow users to search and book flights, hotels, and activities via third-party integrations/APIs.
3. **Collaboration**: The platform must support multi-user trip planning, voting, and shared budgeting.
4. **Mobile Responsiveness**: The platform must be accessible and fully functional on both desktop and mobile devices.

## 5. Scope
**In Scope**:
- Destination discovery and AI itinerary generation
- Flight, hotel, and activity search/booking
- Budget tracking and expense splitting
- Maps, offline modes, and document management
- User profiles, reviews, and notifications

**Out of Scope (for MVP)**:
- Voice Assistant
- AR Navigation
- Smart Wearable Integration
- Carbon Footprint Tracking

## 6. Constraints & Assumptions
- **Constraints**: API rate limits from third-party travel providers, compliance with data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA).
- **Assumptions**: Users will have internet access for initial planning and booking; third-party APIs will provide accurate and timely data.
